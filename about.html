About me

Hi, I'm Achref, and I just graduated from college. I'm a network engineer, but I do a lot more than networks.

In my final year, I did an apprenticeship at a consulting company in Paris, and that's where I fell in love with AWS. The project I worked on there showed me how interesting AWS is, and how important. So I decided to learn it in more depth and build projects of my own, like my Cloud Resume Challenge.

Then came Kubernetes. I'm not usually a fan of group projects, but at college I found a great group of people and we built an amazing project on Kubernetes. The project itself doesn't matter much. What matters is that it introduced me to k8s, and from that moment, I couldn't stop thinking about how it worked and what it could do.

So I started learning again. My college gave me an amazing opportunity to take the CKA exam, and I was thrilled to be selected. When my apprenticeship ended, I dedicated two whole months to intense studying. I didn't just learn Kubernetes, I breathed it. And I did it: I'm officially a Certified Kubernetes Administrator.

Right now

Kubernetes pulled my attention away from AWS for a while. It was great, don't get me wrong, but I still love AWS, so I'm going back to my roots and hoping to pass the AWS Solutions Architect exam soon. I don't know how long it will take, because I'm also looking for a job, so I'm making the most of my time to do both.

This website is where I share projects I've built, ideas, and the simple things I learn along the way.
